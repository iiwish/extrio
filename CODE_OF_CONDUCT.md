# Community code of conduct

Extrio is built through respectful, technically rigorous collaboration.

Participants are expected to be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people,
welcome constructive disagreement, and give others room to learn. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of private
information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
conduct harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the project
maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be handled with discretion and without
retaliation.
